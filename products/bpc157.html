Research overview

BPC-157 is a 15-amino-acid synthetic peptide whose sequence is taken from a larger protein (body protection compound) isolated from gastric juice. Most of the published work is in rodent models of gastrointestinal, tendon, ligament, muscle and bone injury, where it has been studied alongside angiogenic growth-factor signalling[1][2]. A 2025 systematic review found 36 eligible studies, 35 of them preclinical and one clinical, and no clinical safety data[3].

None of these compounds is authorized by Health Canada as a drug. The literature summarised here is preclinical unless stated otherwise, and it is provided as background for laboratory researchers, not as evidence of any effect in people.

BPC-157 FAQ

What is BPC-157?

BPC-157 is a synthetic 15-amino-acid peptide (GEPPPGKPADDAGLV) based on a sequence from body protection compound, a protein found in gastric juice. It is studied mainly in rodent models of tissue injury.

What is the molecular weight of BPC-157?

1419.5 g/mol. Its molecular formula is C62H98N16O22 and its CAS number is 137525-51-0 (PubChem CID 9941957).

What is the difference between BPC-157 and the BPC-157 + TB-500 blend?

This vial contains BPC-157 only. The BPC-157 + TB-500 blend combines it with TB-500, a thymosin β4-derived peptide, in one vial for research designs that study both together.

How is each lot of BPC-157 tested?

Every lot is tested by an independent UK laboratory for purity (HPLC) and identity (mass spectrometry). The lot number printed on the vial matches the certificate of analysis on our lab results page.

How should lyophilized BPC-157 be stored?

Keep sealed vials refrigerated at 2–8 °C, dry and away from light. For storage longer than a few months, many laboratories keep lyophilized peptides frozen at −20 °C. Let the vial reach room temperature before opening to limit moisture uptake.

Is BPC-157 approved for use in people?

No. BPC-157 is not authorized by Health Canada or the US FDA as a drug. It is sold strictly for in-vitro and laboratory research. We do not provide dosing, administration or usage guidance.
