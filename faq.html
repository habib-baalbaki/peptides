Frequently asked questions

Are your products made in Canada?

No. We're a Canadian-based company. Our products are manufactured in a GMP-compliant facility and every batch is independently tested and validated in the UK. We ship them to you from within Canada.

Who tests your products?

An independent laboratory in the United Kingdom. Each batch is tested by HPLC (purity) and mass spectrometry (identity). The lab name, test date and lot number appear on every COA on our UK Lab Results page.

How do I match my vial to its COA?

Every vial has a lot number printed on its label. Find that lot on the product page or the UK Lab Results page and open its certificate.

How long does shipping take?

Orders are shipped by Canada Post Xpresspost with tracking. Most addresses receive their order within 1–3 business days of dispatch. Remote areas can take longer.

Why didn't I get my tracking email?

Some email providers filter messages that mention peptides. Check your spam or junk folder, or contact us with your order number.

How do I pay?

We currently accept Interac e-Transfer. After you place an order request, we email you the payment instructions. Your order ships once payment is received.

Can you advise me on dosing or how to use a product?

No. Our products are sold strictly for laboratory research use. We don't provide dosing, usage, protocol or medical advice.

Are these products approved by Health Canada?

No. These are research compounds. They are not authorized by Health Canada and are not for human or veterinary use.

How should vials be stored?

Store lyophilized (freeze-dried) vials refrigerated at 2–8 °C, away from light. Each product page lists storage details.

What if my order arrives damaged or incorrect?

Email us within 7 days of delivery with your order number and a photo. We'll replace damaged or incorrect items at no cost.
