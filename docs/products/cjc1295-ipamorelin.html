Research overview

CJC-1295 (no DAC), also called Mod GRF 1-29, is the first 29 amino acids of growth-hormone-releasing hormone (GHRH) with four amino-acid substitutions (D-Ala2, Gln8, Ala15, Leu27) intended to slow its enzymatic breakdown. It acts at the GHRH receptor of the anterior pituitary. The name CJC-1295 was first given to this tetrasubstituted peptide with an added maleimido-lysine linker, the Drug Affinity Complex (DAC), which binds serum albumin; in rats that DAC form remained in plasma beyond 72 hours[1]. The peptide supplied here has no DAC linker.

Ipamorelin is a synthetic pentapeptide growth-hormone secretagogue (Aib-His-D-2-Nal-D-Phe-Lys-NH2) that acts at the GH secretagogue (ghrelin) receptor. In its original pharmacology study it released growth hormone from rat pituitary cells, and in rats and swine, with potency similar to GHRP-6. Unlike GHRP-6 and GHRP-2, it did not raise ACTH or cortisol in swine above the levels seen with GHRH, which is why it was described as the first selective GH secretagogue[2].

The two peptides act through different receptors: CJC-1295 (no DAC) at the GHRH receptor and Ipamorelin at the GH secretagogue receptor. Research designs that examine both pathways together use them in combination.

None of these compounds is authorized by Health Canada as a drug. The literature summarised here is preclinical unless stated otherwise, and it is provided as background for laboratory researchers, not as evidence of any effect in people.

CJC-1295 + Ipamorelin FAQ

What is in the CJC-1295 + Ipamorelin vial?

CJC-1295 without DAC (Mod GRF 1-29), 5 mg, and Ipamorelin, 5 mg, in one 10 mg lyophilized vial. For lot XN2601HYP, Janoshik Analytical measured 5.22 mg and 5.10 mg.

What is the difference between CJC-1295 with DAC and without DAC?

Both share the same modified 29-amino-acid GHRH sequence. The DAC (Drug Affinity Complex) version adds a lysine carrying a maleimidopropionyl group that binds albumin in blood, which kept it in circulation beyond 72 hours in rats. This vial contains the version without DAC (3367.9 g/mol); the DAC version is about 3647 g/mol.

Is CJC-1295 without DAC the same as Mod GRF 1-29?

Yes. Mod GRF 1-29, modified GRF (1-29), tetrasubstituted GRF (1-29) and CJC-1295 without DAC are names for the same peptide (CAS 863288-34-0, PubChem CID 56841945).

Why are CJC-1295 and Ipamorelin combined?

They act on two different receptors: CJC-1295 (no DAC) on the GHRH receptor and Ipamorelin on the GH secretagogue (ghrelin) receptor. The blend is supplied for research designs that study both pathways together.

How should lyophilized CJC-1295 + Ipamorelin be stored?
