GH secretagogue research peptides

Growth hormone secretagogues are peptides studied for how they act on the GH axis in laboratory models. They fall into two groups: ghrelin-receptor agonists (GHRPs) such as Ipamorelin and GHRP-6, and GHRH analogues such as CJC-1295 and Tesamorelin. Researchers often study the two classes together, which is why CJC-1295 + Ipamorelin is offered as a blend.
