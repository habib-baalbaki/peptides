Tissue & repair research peptides

This range covers the peptides most often used in preclinical research on tissue repair: BPC-157, a synthetic pentadecapeptide studied in animal models of tendon, ligament and gastrointestinal injury; TB-500, synthetic thymosin beta-4, studied for its role in actin regulation and cell migration; and GHK-Cu, a copper-binding tripeptide investigated in wound-healing and extracellular-matrix models.

Every lot is tested by an independent laboratory (Janoshik Analytical) for purity (HPLC) and identity (mass spectrometry). The lot number printed on each vial matches the certificate of analysis on our lab results page. Orders ship from Canada by tracked Xpresspost, with prices in CAD.
