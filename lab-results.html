What we test

HPLC for purity (% area of the target peak). Mass spectrometry for identity, confirming the molecular weight matches the expected compound. Each report carries the lab's name, the test date and the lot number.

Why the UK?

Independent third-party testing means the lab that verifies the product isn't the one that made it. Our UK testing partner validates every batch before it's released to us.
