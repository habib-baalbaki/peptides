Shipping

We ship within Canada only, by Canada Post Xpresspost with tracking. Orders paid before the daily cutoff ship the same business day. Shipping is free on orders over the threshold shown in the site banner; otherwise a flat rate applies. In hot weather we pack orders with cold packs.

Returns & replacements

For safety reasons we can't accept returns of opened or unsealed products. If an item arrives damaged or incorrect, email us within 7 days of delivery with your order number and a photo, and we'll send a replacement at no charge. If a parcel is lost in transit, contact us and we'll open a claim with Canada Post.

Terms of sale

All products are sold for laboratory and analytical research use only. They are not for human or veterinary use, and are not intended to diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any disease. They have not been authorized by Health Canada. By ordering, you confirm that you are 18 or older and that you'll use the products only for lawful research. We don't provide dosing, usage or medical advice. Prices are in Canadian dollars; GST/HST is applied based on the shipping province.

Privacy

We collect only what we need to process and ship your order (name, email, shipping address). We don't sell your information. Payment details are handled by your bank through Interac. To access or delete your data, email us. This policy is meant to follow PIPEDA and applicable provincial privacy laws.
