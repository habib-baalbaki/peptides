## ⚠️ Read first: regulatory context (this affects the whole business)

- **Apr 9, 2026: Health Canada advisory.** The advisory ["Think twice before injecting peptides bought online"](https://recalls-rappels.canada.ca/en/alert-recall/think-twice-injecting-peptides-bought-online-unauthorized-products-can-seriously-harm) says a **"For Research Use Only – Not for Human Consumption" label does _not_ make an unauthorized drug legal**. Seized products it names include BPC-157, CJC-1295, DSIP, epitalon, GHK-Cu, HCG, ipamorelin, KPV, melanotan I/II, MOTS-c, NAD+, SS-31, TB-500 and retatrutide. That covers most of the catalog every competitor below sells.
- **Jul 29, 2026: permanent injunction against Canlab Research (Québec).** [Health Canada announcement](https://www.canada.ca/en/health-canada/news/2026/07/health-canada-obtains-permanent-injunction-preventing-canlab-researchs-illegal-sale-of-injectable-peptides-in-canada.html). The court barred the company from selling, advertising or helping others sell unauthorized injectable peptides, including through any website reachable from a Canadian IP address. Earlier site-specific warnings named `quadragen.io` and `advancedresearch.bio`.
- **CBSA** is working with Health Canada to stop these shipments at the border.
- Tirzepatide and semaglutide are approved in Canada only as prescription drugs (Mounjaro/Zepbound, Ozempic/Wegovy). Selling "research" versions is exactly what the advisories target.

**Implication:** copying the "research use only" model the market uses is not a legal shield. Talk to a Canadian regulatory or health lawyer before launch. Lower-risk models: cosmetic peptides (GHK-Cu creams, sold as cosmetics under the Cosmetic Regulations), a licensed compounding pharmacy or clinic partnership with prescriptions, or an information/comparison site that doesn't sell drugs.

## 2. UX patterns: what the market does

### Table stakes (every serious competitor has these)
1. **A "Lab Results / COA" link in the main nav.** This is the #1 trust signal.
2. **An announcement bar** with the free-shipping threshold (almost always **$300 CAD**) and the same-day cutoff time.
3. **"Ships from Canada / no customs"** in the hero. Customs seizures are the buyer's biggest fear.
4. **Interac e-Transfer** at checkout. Many sites accept nothing else.
5. **A 10%-off-first-order popup** for email capture.
6. **A research-use-only disclaimer** in the footer and before checkout.
7. **Size × pack-quantity variants** (e.g. 10 mg / 20 mg × 1 / 3 / 10 vials).
8. **Stacks and blends as hero products**: GLOW, KLOW, BPC-157 + TB-500 ("Wolverine"), CJC-1295 + Ipamorelin.

### Differentiators the leaders use

|---|---|---|

### Common pain points (your opportunities)
- **Weak COAs.** Many are "on request", generic, or not matched to a lot. Opportunity: a COA viewer on every product page, matched to the lot, with lab name, test date, and HPLC + MS.
- **Interac-only checkout.** Manual, slow, and confirmation waits on the vendor seeing the e-Transfer. Card, Apple Pay and Google Pay convert better. Note: card processors often refuse this category, which is a real constraint.
- **Dated templates** (Wix and WooCommerce defaults; duplicated carousel text, as on Polar's marquee). A fast, clean, mobile-first build stands out.
- **Inconsistent pricing presentation.** "From $X" hides the price per mg. Show $/mg so buyers can compare.
- **Heat-damaged deliveries** in summer (a recurring complaint). Offer cold packs and say so.
- **No product comparison.** Only Popular has "Compare Compounds".
- **Trust noise.** Unverifiable "Canada's #1" and "20,000+ researchers" claims. Real third-party reviews with order verification would stand out.
- **Tracking emails land in spam** ("peptide" triggers filters; Peptide Warehouse warns about this). Use SMS tracking and a neutral sender name.

## 3. Recommended UX blueprint for our site

1. **Header:** announcement bar (shipping threshold and cutoff), logo, search with autocomplete, Shop (mega-menu by goal and by format), Lab Results, Calculator, Learn, Account, Cart with a free-shipping progress bar.
2. **Homepage:** hero with a concrete promise and one CTA; trust strip (tested / ships from Canada / same-day / secure pay); shop by goal (with counts); best sellers; inline calculator; "How to read our COA"; real reviews; FAQ; newsletter.
3. **Product page:** variant picker (size × qty) showing **$/mg**; lot-matched COA viewer (PDF inline plus key numbers); specs (sequence, MW, CAS, form, storage); "frequently bought with" BAC water and syringes; in-stock status and back-in-stock alerts; shipping ETA by postal code.
4. **Checkout:** guest checkout; Interac plus card and wallets where a processor allows; cold-pack add-on; Canada Post Xpresspost with tracking; clear tax (GST/HST by province).
5. **Account:** order history, one-click reorder, reward points, saved COAs for past orders.
6. **Trust and compliance:** one-domain notice; batch "Verify" lookup; real business address; plain-language legal page. Run the copy past a lawyer, given the 2026 enforcement.
7. **Performance:** fast, mobile-first build, Core Web Vitals green, schema.org Product and Organization markup (Popular already does this well).
